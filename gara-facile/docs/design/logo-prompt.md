## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production logo symbol for the Gara Facile web app, a service connecting businesses with relevant European public tenders.
Primary request: Create a single original, beautifully balanced geometric lowercase g monogram. Its negative space subtly suggests two interlocking matching pieces and an ascending check stroke, expressing the right business finding the right opportunity. Simple and memorable rather than literal government imagery.
Style/medium: precise vector-like graphic rendered as a crisp raster logo, bold broad strokes, solid flat color, restrained Swiss/European typographic sensibility, compatible with Jost typography.
Color palette: the mark itself entirely tangerine orange #F7941D. It will sit on the app's deep navy #1F2B4D sidebar and must also remain clear on white. Transparent negative space, no navy fill in the holes.
Composition/framing: one centered standalone compact square emblem filling approximately 85 percent of a square canvas, generous consistent safe margin. Clear recognizable silhouette at 32px.
Text: no wordmark, no labels, only the abstract lowercase g monogram.
Constraints: genuinely transparent alpha background, no white rectangle, no mockup, no grid, no checkerboard painted into the image, no presentation board, no multiple options, no shadows, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no tiny lines, no stars or EU flag, no stock handshake or building icon, no watermarks. Deliver the finished standalone symbol.
